# Third-party licensing

The project license applies to original BitchPixel source code, not to separately licensed third-party software or assets.

The active development branch maintains a detailed dependency license inventory in `docs/dependency-licenses.md`, distribution notice process in `docs/dependencies.md`, font source and licensing in `assets/fonts/README.md`, and preserved license texts in `assets/fonts/*-OFL.txt` and `docs/licenses/`.

Keep required notices intact when distributing binaries or bundled assets. The development build generates `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` through `scripts/canvas-licenses.py`.

Before merging this proposal, review ownership of the original code, contributor permissions, and compliance with separately licensed content.
