# Contributing to BitchPixel

Thank you for supporting free and accessible digital painting software.

Development is currently taking place on `feature/studio-overdrive-phase-8`. Before contributing, read the development branch's `AGENTS.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`, and `docs/testing.md`.

Open an issue to discuss significant changes, then propose a focused pull request with tests. Preserve accessibility, local artwork, privacy, and cross-platform support.

The intended original-code license is GNU GPL version 3.0 only (GPL-3.0-only); the project owner's copyright and contributor permissions must be confirmed before merging this licensing proposal. Contributors retain copyright in their original contributions and must have the right to license submitted work under GPL-3.0-only. Do not include incompatible or unlicensed third-party code or assets.

Third-party fonts and Rust dependencies keep their own licenses and notices; the development branch documents them in `assets/fonts/README.md` and `docs/dependencies.md`.
