# Contributing to BitchPixel

Thank you for supporting free and accessible digital painting software.

Development is currently taking place on `feature/studio-overdrive-phase-8`. Before contributing, read the development branch's `AGENTS.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`, and `docs/testing.md`.

Open an issue to discuss significant changes, then propose a focused pull request with tests. Preserve accessibility, local artwork, privacy, and cross-platform support.
